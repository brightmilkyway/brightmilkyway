# Hi there 👋
# Yuhao Yuan

I’m an undergraduate researcher and builder at Nankai University working across **digital humanities, computational literary studies, AI, and narrative systems**.

My work explores how literary and interactive worlds can be **represented, modeled, generated, and interpreted computationally**. I’m particularly interested in narrative and event modeling, interactive narrative, and the use of AI in literary and TTRPG contexts.

## Current Work

### White Noise Poem / 白噪诗

A research and development project exploring the intersection of **AI, TTRPG, interactive narrative, and literary computing**.

> 于白噪中写诗 ｜ 探索 AI、TRPG、叙事与文学计算的前沿

The project combines narrative systems engineering with human–AI collaboration, with an emphasis on persistent world state, rule-grounded interaction, event-sourced records, and traceable narrative processes.

**Organization:** `aether-poem`

### Text World Model

An ongoing research project on computationally modeling narrative worlds and their dynamics.

The project studies how interaction histories can be used to represent world-state changes, belief states, and narratively meaningful events, with particular attention to **uncertainty, temporal structure, and formal evaluation**.

### Event-Oriented Literary Modeling

I’m exploring how literary events can be individuated and represented across **temporal, causal, and modal dimensions**, connecting narratology with annotation science, knowledge representation, NLP, and computational literary studies.
